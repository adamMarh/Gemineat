AI engine

On-device model (Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano): checking…

Free, no account, and your cart text never leaves this computer. Needs desktop Chrome 138+, about 22 GB free disk, and 16 GB RAM or a GPU with more than 4 GB VRAM. The model downloads once.

Gemini API (optional, your own key)

More accurate than the on-device model, and works on any computer. Each person uses their own free key; the extension never ships with one.

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. With this engine, cart page text and your numeric targets are sent to Google's Gemini API.
